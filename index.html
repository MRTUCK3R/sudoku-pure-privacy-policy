Privacy Policy for Sudoku Pure

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Sudoku Pure ("the App") is a classic Sudoku puzzle game. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information We Collect

Sudoku Pure does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or postal address to use the App.

The App does not intentionally collect or sell personal information from users.

2. Game Data

Information related to your gameplay, such as game progress, preferences, or settings, may be stored locally on your device so that the App can provide its functionality.

This locally stored information is not intended to identify you personally.

3. Advertising

Sudoku Pure does not display advertisements.

4. Third-Party Services

Sudoku Pure does not intentionally share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

Sudoku Pure is a general-purpose puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

Because the App does not intentionally collect personal information, there is no personal user database maintained by the developer. Any locally stored game information remains on the user's device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Sudoku Pure, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
